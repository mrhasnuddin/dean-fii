DeanFi device: blueprint v0.3

An original hardware wallet derived from the Nano Gen5 form factor. Every control maps to a job in the one-page IA. Proportions are in W (body width): body 1 × 1.5 W, thickness ≈0.15 W, screen 3:4. Draft for approval.

Orthographic views

①Top tabs: section switch. Scroll presses them; clicking scrolls the page. Contra: top1–3
②Left roller: browse projects and events. Mouse wheel over the device only, never the page. Contra: dial
③Confirm (hold) and Back. Contra: ACCEPT / RESET
④Motion switch in Ledger's side-button position; carries over DeanFi2's motion preference. Contra: ON/OFF
⑤D-star coin (engraved, reeded edge) on a 4-link cable chain, hooked to a bar in a bottom-edge slot. Chain physics; tapping it scrolls to the top; jingles.
